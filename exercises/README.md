# Exercises

Each module ends with numbered exercises. Worked solutions live in
[`solutions/`](solutions/), one file per module.

## How to use them

1. **Attempt every exercise before opening the solution.** The value is in the
   struggle; reading a formula teaches you almost nothing.
2. **Type formulas, never paste them.** Argument order has to live in your
   fingers, not in your notes.
3. **When your answer differs from the solution, work out why before moving on.**
   Sometimes yours is better. Sometimes it is right on this data and wrong on
   next month's — that is the case worth finding.
4. **Rebuild the worked examples from a blank sheet** a day later. If you can't,
   you haven't learned it yet.

## A note on the answers

Where more than one approach is reasonable, the solutions show the alternatives
and say which one to ship and why. Where an exercise depends on a judgement call
(how to treat unparseable values, which attrition denominator to use), the
solution states the reasoning rather than pretending there is one right answer.

Numbers quoted in the solutions come from the seeded datasets produced by
`datasets/generate_datasets.py`. If your totals differ, regenerate the data
before assuming your formula is wrong.
