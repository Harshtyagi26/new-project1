## 7. Method of Application

### Step 1 — Surface Preparation (from TDS)
- A clean, sound surface with a good mechanical key is the goal of surface preparation. Dust, plaster, oil, paint, grease, corrosion deposits, and any other harmful materials should all be removed from substrates using a **wire brush, coarse sandpaper or acid etching**.
- The uneven surface should be made smooth or semi-smooth by **scabbling, needle gunning, or grit blasting**.
- Check for cracks in the substrate and fill with **StarShield Putty or Star Water Proof Interior**.
- There must not be any water residue on the surface of the substrate.

*(Landing page version: "Mechanically prepare the surface by grinding or shot blasting to achieve the proper profile for adhesion.")*

### Step 2 — Mixing (from TDS)
- Star Epoxy Flooring shall be **left open for 10 minutes after initial unsealing**.
- Prior to mixing, shake **Part A and Part B** until homogenous, then pour both parts into the mixing container in the ratio **2:1** and thoroughly mix and stir for at least **3–4 minutes** using a slow speed (**300–400 RPM**) drill machine attached with a mixing paddle.
- **Retempering with water or solvent should not be done.** Make sure the surface is dry before application. **Keep stirring during the application.**

### Step 3 — Application (from TDS)
- Pour the mixed epoxy onto the floor and spread evenly using a **notched squeegee**. Use a roller to even out the coating and remove air bubbles. Work in small sections for consistent application.
- During application, the product **needs to be continuously stirred to prevent the particles from settling**.
- The **second coat is applied perpendicularly with an interval of 2–3 hrs, without any dilution**. A third coat might be recommended depending on the substrate.
- Avoid application in direct sun and/or strong wind. Protect freshly applied material from rain etc.
- During adverse weather (high temperatures, low relative humidity, wind, sun) take particular care with curing treatment.
- When working in hot temperature conditions, sprinkled water may be added to the surface to make it stable.
- Clean all tools and application equipment with water immediately after use. Hardened or cured material can only be removed mechanically.
- **DO NOT pond with water before 5 days post application.**

> ⚠️ **Contradiction between sources:** the TDS gives a recoat interval of **2–3 hours** and a maximum time between coats of **36 hours**; the landing page's application step gives no interval at all. Use the TDS figures.

### 8.5 Health & Safety Precautions (from TDS)

- It should not come in contact with skin and eyes or be swallowed. **Protective gloves and goggles should be worn.**
- If contact with skin occurs, wash well with soap and water. Eye contamination should be washed thoroughly with clean water and medical advice should be sought.
- If swallowed, seek medical attention immediately.
- **Star Epoxy Flooring when it becomes damp may release alkalis which can be harmful to the skin & eye.**
- Ensure proper ventilation in the application area.

### 8.6 Handling & Storage (from TDS)

- **Star Epoxy Flooring is flammable.** During handling, keep it away from flammable sources.
- The material should be stored in sealed containers to prevent moisture or contamination from entering, and should be kept closed when not in use.
- In case of spills or leaks during handling, the area should be immediately cleaned up with water.
- Store the product out of reach of children and pets. Normal industrial hygiene procedures should be adhered to.
- **Do not dilute Star Epoxy Flooring while storing.**
- Any expired or unused material should be disposed of properly, following all relevant environmental regulations.
