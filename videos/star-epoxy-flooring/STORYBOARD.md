## Video direction

- **Palette:** Variant B only — near-black `#0a0a0a` ground, `#E5484D` accent (eyebrow + the one
  highlighted word + icons + stat number), `#C1272D` for every divider rule and the end-card
  headline, `#FFFFFF` end card, `#1A1A1A` end-card ink. Never tint a whole headline.
- **Type:** Montserrat 900 uppercase for every headline, Poppins for everything else. No third family.
- **Ground:** every dark scene gets the engineered floor environment — a receding glossy plane in
  perspective, an elliptical specular bloom, a wide radial haze, a 1px horizon hairline and a fine
  grain layer — under the mandatory two-layer scrim (100° diagonal + 180° vertical). Each scene
  varies camera height and bloom position so no two grounds repeat. Frame 2's ground is the one
  matte, unpolished variant.
- **Tick motif:** the 2px gradient line plus 22 tick marks, 56px above the bottom edge, on every
  dark scene and never on the end card.
- **Reserved zones, filled:** the logo lockup sits in the top-right 230x64 on every scene; the
  right 35–40% of Frames 1 and 3 carries the product. Nothing else enters either zone.
- **Motion:** entrances only. Background drift (scale 1→1.08, linear) is the single non-eased move.
  Everything else eases out and settles. The stat number is the only element that gets the spring
  pop. Nothing loops, idles or pulses.
- **Sourcing:** every on-screen number traces to the TDS except the service-temperature range and
  the film thickness, which come from the official product page and are marked with † and a source
  line on Frame 5. No pack sizes and no prices appear anywhere — the live storefront and the TDS
  disagree on both.
