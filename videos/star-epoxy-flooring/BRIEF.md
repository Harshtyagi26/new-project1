## Intent

A short, punchy vertical-of-facts product promo for **Star Epoxy Flooring (Industrial)**
by StarShield Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Dark, cinematic ground with a bold editorial type
system laid over it, closing on a white end card.

The video is built to the client's own **Starshield Product Video Style Guide v1.1**,
which is prescriptive and governs everything: scene flow, palette roles, typography,
layout zones, recurring motifs, and the motion system. The guide is the design spec —
it replaces the workflow's own preset selection.

Tone: industrial, confident, evidence-first. No hype adjectives the spec sheet
does not support.

## Customizations

- **Palette: Variant B — Brand Red / White / Black** (user-confirmed). Near-black
  `#0a0a0a` ground, accent red `#E5484D`, brand red `#C1272D` rules and end-card
  headline, pure white end card, ink `#1A1A1A`.
- **Backgrounds: engineered floor environments** (user-confirmed). Stock photography
  hosts and the HeyGen image catalog are unreachable from this environment, so each
  dark scene gets a built environment — receding glossy epoxy floor plane in
  perspective, specular sheen, reflected product, depth haze, fine grain. Explicitly
  NOT flat color, and explicitly NOT photography: flagged to the client as a
  documented deviation from the guide's "real stock photo" checklist line.
- **Reserved zones filled** (user-confirmed). The guide reserves the top-right logo
  corner and the right 35–40% product zone as empty for later drop-in. The user
  supplied the real logo and real pack shots, so both zones are filled and the video
  ships finished rather than as a template.
- Baseline tick motif on every dark scene, absent on the end card, per the guide.
- Seven scenes: the guide's six-scene template plus the permitted 7th "specs beat"
  inserted between the benefits grid and the how-to-apply scene.

## Notes

- **Sourcing rule is non-negotiable:** every on-screen number traces to the product's
  own Technical Data Sheet or official product page. Two on-screen figures come from
  the official landing page rather than the TDS results table and are flagged as such
  in the delivery: the **−20 °C to 120 °C** thermal range and the **500 µm – 2 mm**
  total film thickness. Everything else is TDS §8.
- **Do not put pack sizes or prices on screen.** The storefront and the TDS disagree
  on every pack size (store: 1.5 / 15 / 30 L; TDS: 1 / 5 / 18.2–20 L), and the 15 L
  variant's selling price currently exceeds its own MRP. Both are live data errors,
  not video content.
- The video is **silent**: the style guide's scene template carries no narration, and
  neither TTS nor the BGM library is reachable from this environment (HeyGen needs an
  interactive sign-in; local Kokoro/MusicGen are not installed). Marked `music: none`
  with no SCRIPT.md.
- Typography is Montserrat 900 uppercase (headlines) + Poppins (everything else). No
  third family.
- No certification/registration wall — the guide names it as noise.
